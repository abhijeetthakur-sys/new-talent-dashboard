The Setup

First — take a breath. You actually did this.

Most people talk about making music their whole lives. You made it and put it out into the world. That matters more than any stream count.

Now the next 30 days are the most important of your release's life.

Streaming platforms like Spotify and Apple Music watch how your song performs in the first few weeks. How many people save it. How many finish listening. How much engagement it gets.

The more it gets early, the more the algorithm pushes it to new listeners. That window is open right now. This guide tells you exactly what to do, day by day, so you don't waste it.

The 30-Day Plan
Day 1
Release Day — Go All In
This is your only chance to have a first day. Make it count.
🎉
Announce on Instagram Stories AND feed
Post the cover art with a simple caption. Don't overthink it. "It's out." with the Spotify link is enough. Add the link in your bio immediately.
📲
Send personal WhatsApp messages to your closest 20 people
Don't blast a group. Text individually — family, friends, classmates. Ask them specifically to listen and save the song on Spotify. Saves matter more than streams.
📌
Pin the release post on your Instagram profile
Your release post should be the first thing anyone sees when they visit your profile today.
🙏
Tag Artium Academy & Artium Originals in your post
We will reshare it. That's extra reach for free, to an audience that loves music.
📡
Share everywhere you exist
Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Status, YouTube Community. Everywhere. Today only happens once.
Days 2–3
The First 72 Hours — Maximum Push
The algorithm is watching. Give it signals.
📊
Check Spotify for Artists
You should have access by now. Note baseline numbers — streams, saves, listeners. Don't obsess, just observe.
🎬
Post a Reel with your song as the audio
Not a lyric video. Just you — singing a line, or a behind-the-scenes moment from recording. Authentic beats polished right now.
💬
Reply to every comment and message
Every person who reaches out — reply. This tells the algorithm your content has real engagement. And it's just good human behaviour.
📩
Ask 5 friends to add your song to a playlist
Playlist adds are a strong signal to Spotify. Ask specifically — "Can you add this to your devotional / Hindi / chill playlist?"
🔁
Reshare anyone who shares your song
If a friend posts about your release, reshare it to your story immediately. Social proof compounds.
Days 4–7
Week 1 — Build the Story
People connect with journeys, not just products.
📸
Post BTS content from recording day
The studio photos, the nervous face before recording, the first playback moment. This content almost always outperforms the official release post.
🎙️
Post a short talking video about the song
30–60 seconds. What the song means to you. Why this composition. Who inspired you. Your face, your voice, no editing needed.
✍️
Share a lyric visual
Put one meaningful line from the song on a clean background. Post it. This drives saves and shares from people who connect with the words.
📬
Submit to playlist curators
Use SubmitHub or Groover to pitch to independent playlist curators in your genre. Budget: ₹500–1,000 for a small batch. Worth it.
📣
Ask your Artium teacher to share
Your teacher has students and followers who trust their taste. A reshare from them reaches a relevant, music-loving audience.
Days 8–14
Week 2 — Go Deeper
Depth creates connection. Connection creates fans.
🎤
Post a live performance clip
Even singing at home with your phone. A live performance of your released song — raw and real — almost always outperforms polished content.
📖
Tell the full story behind the song
A longer caption or carousel post. Where did this song come from? What does it mean? Who is it for? Depth creates connection.
🌐
Share in relevant communities
WhatsApp groups, Facebook groups, Reddit communities for your genre. Not spamming — genuine sharing in spaces where people care about this music.
📊
Review your Spotify for Artists data
Where are listeners from? What's the save rate? Which playlist is sending streams? This tells you where to put energy in Week 3-4.
🤝
Collaborate with another artist
Ask a friend to do a duet Reel using your song. Cross-audience exposure is one of the fastest ways to reach new listeners.
Days 15–30
Week 3–4 — Sustain & Learn
Stay in the game. The algorithm rewards consistency.
🎬
Post one more Reel with your song
Different angle — different line, different mood, different setting. Keep the song active and discoverable.
📻
Pitch to music blogs and YouTube channels
Look for Hindi/regional music blogs, YouTube channels featuring new artists, college radio stations. One feature can bring hundreds of new listeners.
💌
Thank your listeners personally
Go through your DMs and comments. Anyone who said something kind — reply with a genuine thank you. These are your first fans. Treat them like it.
📈
Review your full month data
Streams, saves, playlist adds, follower growth. Write it down. This is your baseline — you'll use it to do better on your next release.
🎵
Start thinking about what's next
The best time to build momentum is when you already have some. Note ideas. Stay in motion. Artists who release consistently grow faster than those who disappear.
The algorithm doesn't reward talent. It rewards consistency and engagement. Your job isn't to make a perfect song — you already did that. Your job now is to make sure the right people hear it.

Your Platforms

What to do where — and why it matters

🎧 Spotify for Artists
Claim at artists.spotify.com. Add photo and bio. Check weekly: streams, saves, listeners, playlist adds. This is your most important data source — check it every week.
📱 Instagram
Your main discovery platform. Post Reels with your song as audio — this is how new people find you. Use the song sticker in Stories. Post at least 3x in your first week.
▶️ YouTube
Your song is on YouTube Music automatically. Also upload a simple lyric video to your YouTube channel — it becomes a permanent, searchable home for the song.
💬 WhatsApp
Most underrated platform for Indian artists. Status updates, personal messages, community groups. Your real network lives here. Use it actively.
🍎 Apple Music
Claim at artists.apple.com. Less data than Spotify but important for international and premium listeners. Add your photo and bio.
📋 SubmitHub
Pitch your song to independent playlist curators, music bloggers, and YouTube channels. Start with 10–15 pitches in your first week. submithub.com
Reading Your Numbers

What your Spotify data actually means

Don't just watch the stream count. These are the numbers that actually tell you how your release is doing.

Metric What it means What to aim for
Streams Times the song was played for 30+ seconds. The most visible number but not the most important. Any number is a start
Saves People who added it to their library. The most important signal you can send to the algorithm. Push hard for saves. 10%+ of streams
Listeners Unique people who played your song. Different from streams — one person can stream many times. Watch this number grow weekly. Grow week on week
Playlist adds How many playlists your song is on. Even one editorial playlist can change everything. Strong algorithmic signal. Even 1 editorial = huge
Save rate Saves divided by streams. Shows how much people who discover your song actually love it. Calculate this yourself. Above 5% = excellent
Source of streams Where listeners found your song — search, playlist, your profile, editorial. Tells you exactly what's working. Know your top source
The Rules

The honest do's and don'ts

Do this
✓Ask people specifically to save the song — not just listen
✓Post consistently — at least every 2 days in Week 1
✓Reply to every comment and DM you receive
✓Share behind-the-scenes content — the journey is compelling
✓Submit to playlist curators early and often
✓Tell the story of the song — why you made it
✓Tag Artium Originals — we actively reshare
✓Be patient — growth takes time and consistency
Avoid this
✗Buy fake streams or followers — it destroys your algorithm standing
✗Post once and go quiet — momentum dies fast
✗Only broadcast — have real conversations with people
✗Compare your numbers to established artists
✗Wait for people to find you — you have to go to them
✗Delete posts because they didn't get enough likes
✗Apologise for promoting your own music — own it
✗Disappear after 2 weeks — stay active and visible
