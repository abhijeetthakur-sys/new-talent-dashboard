Artium Talent Development Programme — Enrolment Agreement

1. Background & Purpose

Artium Academy Private Limited ("Artium") runs the Artium Talent Development Programme (ATDP) to provide identified students with structured grooming, mentorship, and performance opportunities to help them grow as professional musicians. This Agreement formalises the Student's enrolment in the Programme.

2. Programme Benefits

As part of ATDP, the Student will receive:

  • Access to curated masterclasses tailored to their grooming track (Stage, Studio, Social).
  • An assigned mentor matched to their artistic goals and development needs.
  • Personalised growth tracking with monthly mentor inputs and quarterly reviews.
  • Eligibility for internal showcases, Artium Originals, unMute performances, and livestream events — based on readiness and Artium's assessment.
  • Selective marketing support including social media mentions and student spotlight features.

3. Optional Opportunities

The following are offered at Artium's discretion and are not guaranteed entitlements: studio recording sessions, original song releases, mentorship from external experts, guidance for external auditions (advisory only, no guarantee of selection), and personal branding elements.

4. Duration

This Agreement is effective from the Enrolment Date and remains in force for 2 (two) years. Artium may discontinue if the Student stops learning at Artium or breaches any term.

5. Student's Responsibilities

  • Follow Artium's grooming plan and maintain expected standards of learning and practice.
  • Attend all grooming sessions and rehearsals at mutually agreed times.
  • Maintain professionalism in all interactions with the Artium Team.
  • Not upload, share, or distribute any Artium-produced content without prior written permission.

6. Content & Intellectual Property

  • All content produced by Artium or under Artium's direction is owned by Artium. The Student receives appropriate credit.
  • Content created independently by the Student remains the Student's property. Artium may repost or share it for promotional purposes only.
  • Artium-produced content may not be monetised or commercially licensed without Artium's written consent.

7. Exclusivity

This Agreement does not restrict the Student from performing, auditioning, or releasing work with any other organisation. Only Artium-produced content may not be sold commercially without Artium's written permission.

8. No Financial Obligation

There is no additional cost beyond fees already paid to Artium. Any activity involving additional cost will be discussed and agreed with the Student/Guardian in advance.

9. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep all internal programme details and proprietary information confidential during and for 3 years after the term of this Agreement.

10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Mumbai.
